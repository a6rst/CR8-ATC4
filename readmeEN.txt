- Paste the contents from inside of the provided folder into the ATC4 directory.
	!!! OVERWRITE THE FOLLOWING CONTENTS WHEN INSTALLING!!!
		+ atc4\PORT\RJAA\VOICE
			+ All files provided

-------------------- OTHER INFORMATION --------------------


- Due to the game's software limitations, parts of some aircraft may appear transparent (no texture loaded)
	This issue can only be resolved by restarting the game, or rebooting your device, until the issue has stopped.
	SEE IMAGE: DOC-CR8\issue01.png
	
- This product is designed to work in parallel to the original game. Therefore it is vulnerable to any changes made to the original game at the following locations:
	+ atc4\PORT\RJAA\RADAR\fix.ini
